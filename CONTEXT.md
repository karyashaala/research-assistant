# Research Assistant

A research app for exploring questions across subjects, including company filings,
technology, research papers, and maths.

## Language

**Smart Search**:
An optional research mode that asks for clarification when important details are
missing, breaks the investigation into tasks, gathers evidence, checks findings
and disagreements, and combines the results. With Smart Search off, the app
responds directly using web search or what the AI already knows, without first
asking clarifying questions.

**Research browser**:
A browser used within an investigation, where an agent can examine websites and
the user can watch, take control, and hand control back. Saved evidence is part of
the research record; a live tab and its website login are not themselves a saved
research checkpoint.

**Subthread**:
A separate conversation the user opens as a child of another thread to explore a
detail, learn a concept, or investigate a related question; each thread can have
multiple children, and each child can have children of its own, forming a tree.
For each new subthread, the user chooses whether it starts with context from its
immediate parent or without that parent's conversation context, and its side
discussion does not automatically enter the parent's conversation or working
context.

**Parent thread**:
A thread from which one or more child subthreads were opened; it can itself be a
subthread of another parent. Each thread retains its own conversation and can be
continued independently of its children.
